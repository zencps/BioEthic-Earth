Our Mission

To champion the transition toward ethical, human relevant science and build a world where research thrives without wild fauna exploitation.

BioEthic Earth is an academic digital advocacy project created to shed light on the hidden ecological footprint of animal testing. While public discussion often focuses solely on laboratory welfare, our platform examines how biological research demand directly impacts Philippine wildlife and tropical forest ecosystems.

Our Vision

A future where scientific and medical research in the Philippines thrives through modern non animal alternatives, allowing native species like the long tailed macaque to remain protected in their natural habitats.

The Problem

Decades of commercial extraction for laboratory research have contributed to reclassifying the Philippine long tailed macaque as Endangered. Removing native primates from forest canopies severely disrupts seed dispersal and places cumulative stress on wild troops.

Our Approach

Educating students and the public on legal frameworks, exposing the economic drivers behind wildlife trade, and promoting the adoption of human relevant non animal testing alternatives across academic and scientific institutions.
